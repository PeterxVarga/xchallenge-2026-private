## 3. Recommended architecture
HŐTÉR SZÁRNY = one open-sided, passive institutional activity island with repeated bolted/qualified prefabricated frame bays, high-quality solar-blocking overhead cover, optional directional shade wing at ONLY the genuinely exposed side, and independently movable accessible activity furniture. Concept aesthetic: simple light neutral roof, repeated uncluttered frame rhythm, warm colour accent at approved non-climbable panel; legible entrances, replaceable components, no decorative electronics. Example analytical target 6x4 m (24m²), 36m² overhead cover (R170), no child capacity assumed. The intended actual pitched/curved roof, drainage and foundation are to be professionally designed; numerical test uses flat roof only. No drawing dimensions used for fabrication. Wing is optional engineered compatible screen (fixed in approved operating configuration, or supplier-approved removable/retractable assembly only if safe and maintenance justified), NOT loose fabric, ad hoc fastening, or assumed manually movable by children/staff. Keep appropriate supervision/egress and open circulation; check wind, snag/entrapment, fire, visibility, drainage, UV, daylight and thermal conditions. Do not enclose. Existing suitable trees/structure and rearrangement first. Compare (i) overhead-only good commercial shade, cheapest adequate default, (ii) existing compatible shade + directional wing, highest incremental opportunity if actual low-angle residual, (iii) larger overhead roof without side panel, strong countercandidate, (iv) purpose-built distinctive modular pitched/ridge or tensile system only if necessary, (v) mechanical louvers, cantilever, butterfly roof/central drainage or mandatory double skin held unless real extra function warrants complexity. No masonry, rigid coating, water system, PV, sensors-as-product or motor as mandatory. Add R209 high reflectance and optional DUO/REFLECT only if separately measured benefit. Material choice: engineered durable membrane or approved roof/panel; roof material solar/UV properties, ageing, rain/drainage and fire; optional porous side screen sightlines/airflow and actual solar transmission required, no guaranteed match to opaque model. No specified steel dimensions or load ratings. Modular parts must be approved as complete configurations, not universal retrofits or interchangeable across unrelated systems.
