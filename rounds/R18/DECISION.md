## KPI definition and stress test
Primary for Flow: **avoided unnecessary unprotected person-hours during actually required transitions**, sum over comparable required transitions: actual group count x independently logged positive reduction in exposed minutes /60, against current funded existing timetable and ordinary protection; do not increase any other exposure or net loss of meaningful service. Count each child once per interval, no double count; a group delayed/cancelled by unsafe heat is not a project's success unless necessary service is preserved safely. Safety rules have priority. Secondary: proportion of scheduled eligible activities delivered safely (with permitted adaptations; log retained, relocated, cancelled and reason), actual additional protected meaningful activity hours, observed thermal conditions at matched unoccupied sites, total staff time, actual costs, meal access and safe handling, no individual health data. Never add avoided exposure hours to extra protected activity hours if overlapping. Outdoor conditions measure correctly calibrated radiant/globe/WBGT inputs depending on protocol, not air temp claim; no intentionally exposed children or withholding standard protection. Matched weather and similar age-group/activity, use baseline actual comparable ordinary operations with no protocol withdrawal; a representative pretest without children; sample small, no causal health or prevented-illness estimate. 
Model B only: children actually affected per qualifying day × truly avoided minutes/day × qualifying operating days. Three equal seasons, 550k 3y, no forecast:
