Compare passive built shade and tree protection, external window shades (where independently needed), blue-green retention, existing cool rooms, home cooling, public cool network, camp ops, Délben, HŐTÉR FLEX and Árnyéklugas. OASIS/Barcelona precedents, Pécs field PET reduction 9.0°C trees/5.8°C low sails average 9–16, some mature trees/high sails >10°C PET; no local air temp or guaranteed outcome; already in R218, reused rather than novel independent finding. Poissy supplier-authored case max5.1°C indoor compared with older manual shade, different building and condition; reuse principle, not effect guarantee. Stockholm tree water retention and Cartuja Qanat are design leads, not reverified during archival round. Avoid arbitrary rubric scores; prior qualitative matrix is a judgment, not jury prediction.
Recommend HŐUDVAR: Janikovszky historical camp site as proposed starting point; optimized passive shaded activity zone as minimum, retain existing trees, no new capital if funded 2027 tents/HVAC already meet actual need. Existing suitable indoor refuge and safe approved schedule required, outdoor shade is no substitute during severe heat. Long-term greening, water retention, separate external window shading and optional community opening only after local need/permission/cost. Not in first pilot budget. No mist, PCM or powered custom cooling as core. Proposed repeatability is not established adoption.
