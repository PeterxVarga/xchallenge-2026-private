## 1. ROI challenger and decision frame
Question: what incremental appropriate thermal relief does the same money buy versus well-designed shade, ventilation and existing cooled indoor access? Options: A CORE/PRISM higher cooling intensity per seat; B distributed targeted dry airflow + ventilated furniture (BREEZE concept), using existing shade first; C optimized shade over actual use/play area and green canopy; D indoor cooling/hot-day schedule and access measures; E BANK/FLEX storage. B and C/D lead research because reach and reliable appropriate service may dominate greater relief for four seats. A is needed as challenger where no adequate alternative, not discarded. E not justified by seasonal electricity alone (R217). No theme finalization. No health-benefit claim, safe exposure duration or recovery capacity inferred. Separate seated respite service from play/learning service; no common person-hour ranking for different service types.

## 4. New recombination: BREEZE / distributed modular retrofit
Passive airy, shaded, fast-drying seat/back with low-solar-absorption contact surface; avoid dense cushions trapping heat. Optional guarded weather-rated directed ventilation toward seat/back/upper body, airflow selectable by operator/user only where qualified and suitable. Shared external fan vs individual ventilated furniture challenger, no assumed 3.6W transfer. Accessible ordinary shaded places, wheelchair adjacent/alternative air relief, unobstructed sightlines; no accessible moving components, hot surfaces, no restrictive screens, check hygiene/noise/cleanability, no electrical work now. No water mist or compressor required for this concept; does not promise cool air and cannot provide safe prolonged heat exposure. Retrofit EXISTING suitable shade structures first rather than buying full pavilion; group shade / play-area improvement still separate. Optional independently qualified contact/radiant module only if incremental service proven; no mandatory shared hydraulic infrastructure. Reuse fan/seat components rather than custom machinery. Direct active contact + modest airflow can be a separate comparator when appropriate, not automatically higher benefit; hot days may require indoor service rather than more outdoor hardware. No new construction, site or supplier chosen.
