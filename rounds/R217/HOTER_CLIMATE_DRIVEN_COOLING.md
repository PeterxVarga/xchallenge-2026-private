## 5. ROI Challenger and gate
PURE-OPT as universal baseline. CORE-SEAT (direct small qualified chiller + contact, no thermal bank) is engineering/economic lead for reliable active rest if genuinely necessary; no proprietary cooling unit. BANK-FLEX (removable qualified seat storage + night dry-cooler, optional backup or fallback indoor; separate modules) conditional energy autonomy candidate, not default, cannot claim uninterrupted passive coverage. PRISM research higher-performance radiant stage only after comparing incremental useful benefit (not modeled in R217). SKY not default: ~1.5 percentage points above large storage in corrected overall scenario but worst streak12 vs11, uncertain flux and cost. Alternative PV-assisted direct active cooling only compare if grid unavailable/energy constraint, not add PV/battery simply for novelty. Simpler adequate shade/fan or cooled indoors wins if no meaningful extra service. Next highest ROI: challenge actual comfort benefit of contact/radiant vs well-designed shade/fan using published matched experimental evidence and full safe-service cost, evaluate qualified compact direct active and modular removable options; no physical prototype. If site-specific wind/solar or recharge decision changes winner, revisit actual station data, local conditions and plausible performance curves. Theme open.
