## 4. Engineering falsifier computations (new numerical screening, not calibrated site models)
4.1 Time mismatch: at 5K permitted assumed water rise, cp=4.186 kJ/kgK, 4kW needs ~0.191 L/s. For six-hour cooling 4.128 m3 throughput.
