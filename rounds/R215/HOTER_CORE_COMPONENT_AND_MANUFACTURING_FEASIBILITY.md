## 3. Design simplification and manufacturing route
CORE Bench: one compact replaceable professionally engineered seat cassette/heat spreader assembly on a normal approved seating system, four example independent places (no capacity rating), optional separate radiant surfaces in phase2. Heat source (A) existing suitable authorized spare plant capacity and isolated qualified interface only if verified; (B) manufacturer-approved warm-liquid recirculating commercial system installed in separate weather-protected ventilated service location, with complete installed rated conditions/warranty; no custom refrigeration or proposed retrofit on an arbitrary chiller. Conventional heat-transfer plate with attached hydronic tube/meanders and corrosion-compatible heat spreader is likely lower integration/initial tooling effort for a single engineered unit; roll-bond custom manufacture promising in repeats but quote/scope necessary, not assumed cost savings. No DIY fabrication, pressure ratings, material thickness, assembly detail or use of exposed coolant tubing. Qualified supplier determines safe contact surface, cleanability, leak containment, moisture, accessible clearances, fire/electrical and refrigerant compliance, water chemistry, winterization, insulation, inaccessible equipment, safe noise and heat rejection, repair/replacement and full assembly acceptance. Simple local responsible controls and fault/condensation response; not children controlling device or automated medical safety. Use only appropriate conditions with independent adequate indoor option for everyone; no active system makes an otherwise unsuitable day safe. No direct water supply or spray requirement for closed-loop operation, but initial fill and maintenance fluid necessary. No evidence of local spare chilled source or ability to share.
CORE Cartridge alternative: sealed professionally approved moderate temperature thermal-storage seating cassette with recharge outside occupied zone, avoids live plumbing/electric in seating module; no exposed cold packs, food refrigerator assumption or DIY. Material/cycle/thermal contact and condensation safety, staff handling and approved separate recharge equipment are decisive. Compare short actual sessions or naturally cool charging opportunities, not all-day assumed service. Existing shade/AIR and indoor rest remain strong equal-function alternatives.
