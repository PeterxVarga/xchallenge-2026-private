# LOMBŐR – HŐVÍZ 2.0
**Protect the trees we already pay to plant, with the available water.** A 2027 incremental pilot for young street trees: low-tech calibrated root-zone measurement + volume logging + operator-approved irrigation prioritisation. Not new water extraction, new irrigation app, blanket water cuts, or promise of 1800 treated trees. Municipal KŐKERT/Hivatal is proposed operator only. No claim of owner agreement.
## Design
Stage A up to HUF 350k inclusive of the later 2.2m cap: request existing tree inventory/age/species, irrigation rounds, volume, water source and restrictions, crew/trip costs, tree loss/vitality, current methods; check permissions; four reference sensors and observation only if helpful. Compare current process with an improved simple weather/rain + manual field rule BEFORE expanding sensors. No new software server or autonomous valve controls. If log quality or safe/meaningful opportunity not present, stop.
Stage B target 120 consenting/approved public trees: 12 comparable clusters of 10, form six matched pairs by species, planting age, exposure, pit, soil, water source and maintenance. Allocate one cluster per pair to test (60), other current care (60), randomized if operator permits. Baseline 2–4 weeks, 12 representative monitoring points x two depths = 24 sensors max, calibrated by horticulturist to suitable soil range; manual devices require service, dry-range limitations, recording time, vandal protection. Can replace with better technology only after quotes. Comparison never under-watered or denied normal care. Trained crew reads and records, rain/weather and crew observations; rule gives provisional priority, crew decides. Missing/stale/discordant readings -> no skip; fallback current approved care. No universal thresholds. Meter actual delivered water, trip time/distance/cost and rain. Record soil water availability/threshold exceedances, visible stress/condition, survival through season and next spring. Increased water to truly stressed trees is allowed: if water use rises report it transparently, not savings.
Primary: measured m3 per treatment tree vs matched current-care control, conditional on no worsening of pre-agreed agronomic stress/vitality criteria; also operator hours/trips and missed critical watering. 15% reduction proposed as test objective not forecast or validated statistically powered target. Six cluster pairs exploratory small-sample; report pair differences/uncertainty, no claims of significant improvement unless adequate data. Separate heat benefit: literature reports crown microclimate, locally no pedestrian cooling measurement; canopy preservation longer-run proxy, not immediate neighborhood temperature claim. No personal data; mark tree IDs, not residents.
## Budget and economic honesty
350k Stage A is inside 2.2m maximum planning budget, not additional. Stage B only after signs of marginal value and real offers; remaining provisional 1.85m. 24 x 23500 = 564000 proxy manual tensiometer budget (vendor listing, not comparable full quotes), design 200k, logging 180k, installation/protection 240k, crew/recording 420k, workflow 100k, evaluation 200k, reserve 296k. If instrument unsuitable, fewer points/different quote or stop. No assumption of free crew/greywater, no extraction rights, no change to water source. Quote approved meters, sensors, labour, tax and recurring upkeep. 3-year TCO conservative pilot plan incl 350k annual maintenance each extra year; not scaling cost. Unit savings alone unlikely to justify pilot: model includes three scenarios and break-even threshold. Scaling figures are hypothetical, require separate scalable cost forecast and verified comparable cohorts. No monetized avoided mortality or arbitrary tree asset value.
## Implementation
2027 Q1 permissions and data/Stage A, Q2 matched clusters and approved measurement, Q3 field pilot through summer, Q4 independent evaluation, next spring survival check. Operator/horticulturist signs limits, no change to minimum care, fallback. Scale only with verified cost per tree, measured outcome and confidence, actual staffing, agreed marginal benefits > actual recurring and amortized cost or explicit public-value funding. No money spent now.
