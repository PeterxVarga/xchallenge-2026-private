## LAKÁSFORDULÓ Lite — actual homes, not dashboard
Propose 720k gross first-year allowance for one controlled batch of 20 routinely re-lettable, currently or newly vacant municipal units with legitimate allocation authority and independently funded normal readiness work. No owner agreement or available batch confirmed. Phase A max 150k included: reconcile latest existing portfolio and ASZ action plan, identify stage delays, current workflow, compare existing repair and letting timeline baseline (12 months), check rules, stock rights, public eligibility and rental demand; if no batch or costed bottleneck stop. Phase B: no proprietary software/new database, use approved existing records; pre-plan inspections/materials and schedule already-required works when lawful, parallelize only independent administrative tasks, one standard safe handoff checklist and weekly blocked-case review. No bypass of tenancy selection, safety, accessibility, required repair, legal or data-access safeguards; no eviction or forced departure incentive; no preferential allocation, no automatic decisions or surveillance. Existing eligible waiting list allocation remains unchanged. Categorize vacancy (major repair, legal restriction, approved sale, ready soon, ready); exclude other categories unless their own lawful scope. New repairs beyond normal funded pipeline explicitly cost separately; if unknown funding, project HOLD. Budget 150k diagnosis, 180k staff handoff coordination, 120k technical inspections, 100k workflow/training, 100k evaluation, 70k reserve =720k. Provisional incl VAT and loaded labour, no quote. Actual physical works not magically free; recognize only incremental acceleration where independently approved base works unchanged. Stage A inside ceiling.
KPI key-to-key calendar days, repair-to-ready, ready-to-let, signed move-in and safe habitable days brought forward; same-scope matched historic/comparison cohort, exclude legally unavailable, record timeouts, case mix/season/rights. Sample 20 not powered for robust general causal claim. Economic benefit: actual collected rent brought forward minus variable occupancy/repair/service costs, plus only demonstrable avoided vacant-only costs, never double count. Extra earlier days = N*d; not permanent additional homes or new revenue forever. Social metric additional occupied housing-days (if actual move-in), not unique homeless people reached.
