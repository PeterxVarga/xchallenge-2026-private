## 5. Döntés és kritikusút
Three independent ways to win. Rubric evidence (not numeric jury simulation or prediction):
| Criterion | A HŐTÉR FLEX | B HŐOTTHON 365 | C HŐJÁTÉK |
| Local relevance & impact | strongest repeated historical local service disruption, 2027 gap unknown | local renovation channels plus Hungarian/Óbuda evidence; actual hot room unknown | explicit local shade demand already funded; contact/active play residual unknown |
| Function & novelty | actual full service, capacity and hot-transfer reduction | physically improved occupied room plus enduring affordability, no generic Budapest-first claim | child-level heat/contact and active use in specific play zone, no generic oasis |
| Feasibility | existing operator; food, capacity, permissions | household and building consent, compatible installation, time | technical/safety permission, real use zone and existing canopy |
| Costs & sustainability | same-service lean comparator, no hidden labor, full M2 unknown | electricity stress, no hypothetical subsidies | net used-hour not nominal, maintenance, optional premium only after effect |
| Deployment & measurement | actual 2027 operational baseline, net exposed time & maintained service | occupied room-hour & degree-hour/IAQ/energy | protected active use, contact/radiation & occupancy |
A remains provisional first validation priority (not winner frozen). B more concrete beyond generic advice, but precedence is also a novelty counterargument; C plausible physically distinct third, adult HŐPONT lower because neighboring urban precedent and active cost. Cross-sector learning not gratuitous bundle. Red team iteration: (1) if all ordinary 2027 camp works implemented and service sufficient, no extra A; B/C compete. (2) if AC procured but unaffordable or exterior shade disallowed, B re-scope to suitable actual eligible room or drop; no unpaid operating promise. (3) if existing shade enough or extra sitting displaces normal play, reject C or improve a distinct contact hotspot, not increase area. (4) if ordinary solution wins, do not purchase high-tech for novelty. (5) only compare provided equivalent outputs, no totalization person vs child vs degree hours, no SROI mortality. 
Decision-critical input checklist, not outreach: A current approved venue, actual concurrent children and staffed usable cool places, meal and travel workflow, ordinary planned shade/cooling status; B approved DEA excerpt/owner separate approval and real occupied room thermal/affordability gap; C ordinary installation and concrete occupied hotspot, activity + operator + consent. Time gate independent 2027 operation, not multi-year works. Public site searches did not resolve these. No authorized direct contact, measurement or purchase. Extra broad search ROI lower than these inputs. Continue only research genuinely decision-changing facts under current scope; no forced theme freeze.
