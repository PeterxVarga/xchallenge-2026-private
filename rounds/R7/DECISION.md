## ZÖLDKÖR design — no new shredder
Stage A 300k of project cap only: map batch origins/contaminants, obtain current intake/rejection and cost records, verify holder/operator permits, legal treatment/receiver options (waste act + concession scope), obtain written quality tolerances and indicative pricing from authorized recipient(s), test representative samples with professionally approved method. Compare source segregation and adjustment of existing sorting first, rental screening second; no speculative purchase. No outreach or agreement today. Mixed waste/contaminated residues not presumed biomass product; waste status does not automatically end through chipping; no unapproved fuel combustion. Test moisture, particle size distribution, contaminants, ash or other buyer-specific properties via qualified lab; use buyer contract spec, not generic ISO claim. Matched input lots, mass balance in tonnes on consistent moisture basis, no use of m3 to tonnes conversion without density; log fines/oversize reject routes, labor energy transport and gate fees. Measure accepted compliant tonnes and incremental net margin versus current route; no double counted 8–10m historical savings. Stop if 2026 gap resolved, no legal receiver, no feasible quality or positive marginal economics.
Provisional 2.4m first year incl stage A: sampling 250k, legal/offtake assessment 150k, sorting/handling 350k, screening service rental/transport 650k, lab 250k, labour 450k, contingency 300k. Stage A included, allocations overlap planned line items not extra: 100k each baseline, sampling, legal. All gross allowances not quotations. 300k/year incremental fixed operation y2/3 illustration; 3m three-year fixed TCO. Variable production and logistics cost not included again as fixed if already in net margin; in real model split one-off pilot expenditure vs per-tonne variable costs, reconcile overlap before approval.
