## Decision and falsification
Janikovszky first on direct residual historical evidence + repeated dual-use and 91.17% non-host weekly registration slots. Most suitable actual geometry not verified. Széchenyi second (new shared campus); Harmat third (implementation readiness/partnership hypothesis); Szent László fourth (alternative service hub, may outrank if entire camp can run there and eliminates transfers); Fekete reference (already implemented). Ranking reflects evidence, not predicted heat or jury scores. Existing tent/HVAC as strong comparator; if equal service lower cost, no new pavilion. If relocating camp provides sufficient safe capacity at lower overall cost with equivalent access/meals, assess that before physical capex. No invented exact footprint; candidate: outside sport playing area, next to existing suitable activities, no encroachment on paths/trees, approved clearance/utility/structural/fire/access check. First test 40–60m² net useful shade module (hypothesis only, not measured or engineered) vs reuse + distributed shade; 80–120 m² not yet justified. Compare actual shaded activity area at 10/12/14/16 summer times (geometry + surface + actual occupancy; no unsafe pediatric trial); existing protected indoor capacity independent. If no verified gap, hold. No live outcome; end-to-end/site/full readiness UNKNOWN.
