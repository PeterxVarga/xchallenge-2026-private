## HŐOVI phased design — not a generic audit
Single municipal programme, maximum 2 matched-priority sites at first. Existing stock and normal approved works remain baseline; no unnecessary separate application. Phase A 250k planning allocation: ask KEÓ/Vagyonkezelő for actual summer open sites, activity spaces, delivery and shade asset plan, attendance/time-of-day and heat-related programme modifications; compare current shade positions by sun path, safe wind/installation, rain drainage, surface risks and indoor fallback. Pick a currently unaddressed zone with sufficient affected children-hours and no conflicting planned works. If none, STOP. If repositioning existing canopy or schedule achieves same result and is safe, use that not new procurement. Phase B first site, then second only if demonstrated gap and quoted fit. Target certified/engineered shade element at a high-use play/activity area with independent installer/structural and child safety checks, clear egress/access, operation limits, site permissions, no exposure of children to unsafe control, ordinary heat rules remain intact. Select actual footprint and product from quote, not guessed m2. Existing shade and safe indoor rest continue. No intentional child heat exposure, no personal/health data collection.
Budget gross planning ceiling HUF 3.4m, no official cap: 2 × 900k installed allowances (historical Kékvirág gross 697555 old site-specific quote plus uncertainty, not current quote); 250k baseline; 220k shared calibrated monitoring; 300k additional site adaptation not included in supplier installed price (remove if overlaps); 230k staff/training/evaluation, 600k contingency. 3y fixed total 3.84m with 220k annual additional operation in y2/y3. If one site, cost independently quoted, do not assume half. For a single first site, working ceiling 1.85m incl 900k installed, 200k baseline, 150k monitoring, 150k site adaptation, 150k staff/eval, 300k reserve. Alternative existing-stock-only 350k if equal added value; no coverage assumption. No committed funding.
Measurement: simultaneous paired unoccupied points under new shade and comparable existing conditions; calibrated black globe/radiation measurements and weather/wind, validated index appropriate to purpose (record actual WBGT or calculated PET only with correct inputs; not interchangeable); no air-temperature claim. Pre/post comparable weather and occupancy counts aggregate, matched schedule/zone; added safe usable child-activity time versus existing/funded baseline, recorded by staff without individual tracking, no new hard heat threshold, operator-approved rules; substitution from already adequate shaded area is not additional value. Track maintenance, operating hours and incidents. Spring baseline and safe commissioning by May, June–Aug use conditional on approval and procurement; year 1 evaluation, 2–3y optional rollout only with evidence.
