## 3. Alternative challenge
| Alternative | Value | Critical weakness / decision |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary approved schedule, existing shade and maintained AC | minimal intervention, immediately relevant | if solves 2027 gap, wins; no automatic extra spend |
| High-reflectance opaque white conventional membrane or already similar cover | mature material and direct solar shielding | comparator optical, UV, thermal and assembly properties needed; if matches premium effect at lower cost, wins |
| PDRC membrane on compatible approved structure | reduces solar absorption and emits heat to sky, potentially lower underside radiant exposure with no powered cooling during operation | premium price and local performance unknown; weather, sky view, ageing, wetting, outgoing IR/underside emissivity and geometry matter |
| Ventilated double-layer roof | can reduce heat transfer from upper canopy to underside | harder structural/wind, ventilation and cost; cannot stack layers blindly or impair PDRC sky view; credible conventional challenger if properly designed |
| PV roof with fans | electricity/other uses | hot underside, electrical/weatherproof/structural approval, cost and demand, no proven superiority for 2027 target |
| Mist/evaporative ceramic | can add cooling in favorable weather | existing mist gate; water stress, humidity, drift, hygiene and maintenance. No reuse of unapproved water for aerosols |
| Active radiant shelter (Cold Tube/KlimaKover), chilled-water loop, PCM | innovative transfer mechanism, specific possible benefits | power, dewpoint/condensation, novelty not net value, complexity, no local installed quote; not first pilot |
| Ventilation/solar control + correctly sized indoor cooling | higher resilient capacity in severe heat and shoulder seasons | heritage, ventilation and actual room capacity unknown; independently evaluated M2 not replacement by outdoor shelter |
Ordinary white/high-performance conventional alternative is the strengthened comparator, not unshaded asphalt or dark old roof. Retain no-additional-project branch. Existing 2027 funded/actual shelters and cooling assessed FIRST. The PDRC concept is the highest-interest incremental technical challenger, not selected construction. A robust no-regret solution uses authorized program/meal/space coordination first and only upgrades material when verified extra effect per resource and service continuity justify it.
