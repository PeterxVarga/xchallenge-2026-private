## 2. Product architecture — one concept, selectable envelope
HŐTÉR is a usable heat-protected activity island, not a separate app or generic shade-product catalogue. Aim: keep an actual planned child activity accessible in appropriately assessed summer conditions with passive reduction of radiant exposure and no unnecessary relocation. Proposed first site Tekergők camp, if unmet local need remains after normal HVAC/shade works. One example module 36m² nominal overhead cover around a 6x4m activity target (R170 illustrative only); no fixed shape, height, occupancy, structural plan or promise of area per child. Choose existing suitable structure, commercial engineered standard new system or professionally qualified retrofit. Core: (a) actual use geometry, including occupied zone and exposed perimeter; retain natural circulation, sightlines and accessible route; (b) good solar-blocking, highly reflective, appropriately UV-rated and documented durable material; (c) protect the floor and near radiant surroundings by location and shade timing, not by unproven coating or new surface by default; (d) simple climate/use measurements and explicit operating limits; (e) independent adequate indoor fallback for all, supervised operations. Optional replaceable roof technology only when the whole assembly permits it. No loose layers, DIY attachments or unapproved geometry. No pump, water mist, PV, automatic actuators or custom software in base. Passive structure does not consume active cooling electricity, but total service is not zero-energy.
Three alternatives at SAME useful function and verified structural/UV/safety gate: standard high-quality single layer (reference and default), upper-side high-reflectance surface on suitable rigid roof (candidate if actual matched benefit), separately engineered two-layer canopy (candidate only after good-standard benchmark). A one-layer asymmetric low-emissivity underside is another known material mechanism, not required. Shade area, thermal effect and installed cost must be assessed separately. No duplicate ordinary 2027 provision. Strong conventional case first; if actually sufficient choose it or do not build; if no safe independent indoor fallback in severe heat solve accommodation first.

## 4. New self-consistent steady-state thermal model
Executed JS numeric bisection (80 iterations on [-30,150] Celsius, sigma=5.670374419e-8). Opaque horizontal thin plate(s), 1m² normalized, equal shade footprint assumed, no transmitted shortwave, no thermal storage/conduction to support or full comfort, no body/ground dynamics. Grey diffuse broadband infrared incl effective sky temperature, not spectrally resolved PDRC. R(x,y)=sigma*((x+273.15)^4-(y+273.15)^4). Equilibrium single alpha*G=ht*(T-A)+hb*(T-A)+eu*R(T,sky)+ed*R(T,env). Double ideal upper opaque, lower no direct solar, gap air assumed ambient with independent convective coupling hg on both surfaces, lower top emissivity eg, upper underside .9, E=1/(1/.9+1/eg-1). Upper alpha*G=(ht+hg)*(Tu-A)+eu*R(Tu,sky)+E*R(Tu,Tl). Lower E*R(Tu,Tl)=(hg+hb)*(Tl-A)+ed*R(Tl,env). Solve lower for each upper. This exchange is internal consistent; gap ventilation not physically modeled, no claim these coefficients represent actual built gap. Under-roof effective LONGWAVE-only temperature at view factor F=.3: (F*ed*(Tl+273.15)^4+(1-F*ed)*(env+273.15)^4)^.25-273.15, Tl=T for single. Fixed uniform nonroof environment; low-e reflects that environment. No sky open fraction, diffuse/reflected solar or directional effects; not MRT including solar, actual body, WBGT, UTCI, PET, air temperature or safety. Actual child heights and view factor vary.
