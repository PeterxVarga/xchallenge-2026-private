## 3. New hybrid architecture and competitive alternatives
HŐTÉR HŐPUFFER working engineering name, not final brand: a dry, passively shaded and precooled (in sense of avoiding solar warming, NOT machine refrigeration) activity island. Four mechanisms: (1) overhead high quality opaque or appropriate UV-rated solar-blocking roof; (2) select ground/zone that is shaded hours BEFORE planned use, using existing tree/building shadow or appropriately placed approved structure; account for surrounding hot facades/pavement as well as occupied patch; (3) directional compatible wing only for actual low-angle solar gap, choose alternate orientation/open sides to preserve airflow, no unverified wind claim; (4) align age-appropriate activity, simple environmental checks and actual measured service. Optional vegetation only sustained by approved existing landscape programme; no irrigation added by default. No mandatory new flooring: compare existing earlier-shaded zone and equivalent ordinary shade first. If material replacement necessary, require accessibility, drainage, cleanability, slip/contact, play surface compliance and maintenance; no outdoor loose mats, improvised reflective surfaces or experimental PCM near children. All children retain approved suitable indoor alternative in severe weather. This cannot cool outdoor air reliably below ambient.
Technology tournament: A good conventional fixed shade in already suitable area (strong baseline; stop if adequate); B early ground shade + select naturally shaded surroundings (advance, low extra intervention if existing geometry); C compatible low-angle wing with open cross-flow (advance only for actual sun gap, compare roof extension); D light roof/qualified reflective or double layer (optional separate matched comparison per R209); E passive wind catcher/stack chimney (hold: no wind guarantee, obstructed flow and heat load; compare unobstructed sites first); F dry sensible thermal mass/PCM regenerating at night (research reserve, weight/charging); G film plus liquid radiant loop/ground exchanger (hold for future engineered controlled operation); H water/evaporative or wetted porous material (hold, consumption, hygiene, humid heat and historical local water restriction); I low-energy fan (separate active alternative only if appropriate professionally assessed conditions, not base passive requirement or substitute for indoor fallback). Innovation hypothesis is use-zone thermal history + low-angle protection + preserve ventilation and measure actual service compared to GOOD conventional layout, locally adapted; known mechanisms, not patent novelty.
