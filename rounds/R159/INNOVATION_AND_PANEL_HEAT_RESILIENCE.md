## 4. HŐCSERE — két hasznos hőoldal
Original KŐHŰTŐ needs verified cool water as sink; alternate mechanism: take actual unwanted heat out of an occupied room and simultaneously deliver useful hot water. No new consumption created to justify cooling, no using drinking water as dump, no claim that condenser heat is free (work required, lift and efficiencies). Existing ASHRAE Handbook S17 (HTML) recognizes air-water HPWH in kitchens/laundries/pools, sensible/latent extraction and useful hot water; compare normal heat exchange first, concurrent source and sink profiles, total operating costs. This is established engineering transferred to a specific public service, not patent invention. 
S8 asset manager 2025 text explicitly lists cooling opportunity built in Apró Csodák, Napsugár, Apraja Falva, Manóvár main kitchens; PDF screenshot failed DisabledError (attempted). Earlier R154 childcare report also describes existing HVAC and kitchen works; original re-open unavailable here. Existing infrastructure is strong counterevidence to generic cooling retrofit. The candidate is one needful occupied supporting kitchen/laundry/24h care room, with real heat exposure and compatible hot-water load, not an upgrade of all four kitchens or assumed connection to playground. If existing normal cooling is sufficient, no project; energy-only change without direct heat-adaptation outcome not eligible per R11. Occupational safety, existing food-safe ventilation and hygiene cannot be reduced. Potential 24h care/user benefit only if useful occupied zone has evidenced heat burden and appropriate separation, not raw kitchen exhaust directed to residents. 
H0 ordinary functioning cooling/ventilation plus existing hot water. H1 factory-rated, professionally selected heat-pump water heater extracting heat from appropriately suitable occupied/support area, additional cooling as actual comfort output and necessary dedicated backup HVAC retained. H2 rated simultaneous heat recovery chiller/HP or existing compatible approved factory heat recovery terminal, separate potable/service water heat exchange, no DIY modification of refrigerant circuit, no bypass of safety or temperature control. H3 larger central plant/shared network deferred if new engineering needed and time/cost high. An exhaust or wastewater heat exchanger may save fuel but does not independently reduce occupied heat in ordinary operation, therefore not eligible by itself.
