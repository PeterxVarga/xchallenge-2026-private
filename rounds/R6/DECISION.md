No prediction-driven restriction of child meal entitlement or nutritional portions. If existing process meets need, stop. Pilot ceiling 650k gross including stage A; 2-4 schools, example 600 actual lunch portions/day, 100 measurement days; baseline and matched comparison with menu/calendar/age, 10-20 day baseline, stepped start; secure food-safe weighing, aggregate counts only, simple forecast baseline vs existing ordering using historical school-day counts; caterer-approved adjustments only, separate unavoidable and avoidable waste, record returned unserved food vs plate waste and actual procurement or production reduction; dietitian review; no personal/student tracking or reduction of needed food.
