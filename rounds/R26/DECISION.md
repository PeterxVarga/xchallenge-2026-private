## Frozen concept, not an app / not a lunch delivery project
**HŐTÁBOR – Hőségálló napközi: két helyszín, egy összehangolt hőségnapi szolgáltatás.** The actual proposed intervention is a pre-authorized alternative operating mode of the existing municipal day camp: preserve meaningful programme, meals, rest and safe outdoor opportunity while avoiding necessary hottest-time outdoor transfers. A site- and time-specific capacity plan uses the existing protected infrastructure first. If school-only or ordinary rescheduling solves the gap without degrading services, use it. If the cultural centre programme remains necessary and lunch is the bottleneck, provide same-site approved lunch service there on selected days, not permanent canteen construction. No mandatory new software, AC, tents or hardware. The facility branch is a concrete candidate not a promise. New local value is integrating capacity, programme and essential-service continuity with measured marginal reduction in exposure. Not world-first scheduling/transport. No confirmation the operator lacks an equivalent new plan.

1. One authorized room/route/activity inventory: capacity including adults; occupancy/availability/time; ventilation, safe sun/weather restrictions; qualified supervision and accessible ingress/egress; existing approved dining/handwash/rest spaces. One actual camp programme, two alternate day plans (ordinary and heat-day, both with all current protections), preapproved room bookings and provider call-off where possible. Verify 2027 real use and actual need before any spending beyond baseline review.
2. At forecast review before the provider's actual agreed cutoff, designated camp lead checks official alerts and facility availability, expected occupancy, planned activities, current risk and required ordinary precautions. No self-created temperature safety threshold. Reschedule or use school-based equivalent first; only for remaining necessary heat-day culture-centre group use the approved co-located meal branch if all gates pass. Check again at start and before any transfer. No model overrides staff, health, food or safety decisions; if unsafe, use approved fallback or suspend unsafe activity. Parent information via existing lawful channel. No extra charges due to pilot.
3. Meal path only if approved: actual contracted qualified provider prepares approved meals including required diets, transports/handles under valid hygiene and food safety process, delivers to authorized receiving/serving arrangement, records required temperatures/times and traceability, clean/dirty separation and waste, maintains supervised waiting and programme for all groups. Existing dining location remains fallback, provided safe; unsafe route never forced. No ad hoc catering bypass, unauthorized room conversion, required unbudgeted kitchen construction or mandatory packaging waste. Actual approval requirements assessed with provider/authority.
4. Daily aggregate log: baseline counterpart, number of affected children, necessary transfer segments and exposed minutes (net over whole day), planned vs delivered equivalent meaningful programme minutes, entitled meals provided and dietary requirements fulfilled, extra labour minutes and cost, room conflicts and safety incidents. No child-level health data, camera, location trackers, separate app or unnecessary sensors. Shared calibrated indoor temperature/humidity and optional outdoor reference at unoccupied locations where useful; not air-temperature reduction claim or physiological/health dose.
