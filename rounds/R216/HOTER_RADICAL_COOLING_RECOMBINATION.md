## 3. Recombination and conditional ranking
PURE-OPT: passive shade with exterior solar reflectance/high sky emissivity, separated ventilated roof, shaded underside radiative shielding if useful, open cross-ventilation, no solar glare or greenhouse effect. Baseline matched against optimally designed conventional shade, not no shade.
BANK (new cost/complexity challenger): four hypothetical rest places, sealed manufactured hydronic/thermal-storage seat modules only if safe/cleanable, regeneration by cool night air/remote sky heat rejection, optional shared central qualified chiller backup; no continuous water spray, no owner-built pressure/cold electronics, no daily manual swapping assumed. For independent direct contact service only. PCM inside guarded replaceable module with qualified thermal coupling; 28 C material not a safe contact setpoint, seat surface could be warmer. If hot nights, reject passive-only guarantee; a conventional bench or standard active seat may be better.
PRISM (maximum potential): combine improved roof, near side panels and optically tested semi-transparent LW reflectors, membrane only where humidity and extra radiant benefit justify; seat optional, air access and sightlines hard gate. No requirement to integrate every technology. Reflector and membrane have opposite functional IR requirements; not same film. Membrane lowers accessible cold panel temp but can attenuate radiation and may increase chiller burden; no validated energy model.
SKY (experimental extension): external above-roof sky-facing fluid heat rejection/storage loop, compare with night air radiator, no mandatory sky collectors. Do not confuse passive reflective coating with useful fluid cooling capacity; no heat dump near occupants. All child-facing use conditional on qualified design. No prolonged outdoor exposure justified by model.
